Problem Statement Medium
LeetCode 340

Given a string s and an integer k, find the length of the longest contiguous substring that contains at most k distinct characters.

Example 1: Input: s = "aababbcaacc", k = 2 → Output: 6
The longest substring with at most two distinct characters is "aababb" (length 6).
Example 2: Input: s = "abcddefg", k = 3 → Output: 4
The longest substring with at most three distinct characters is "bcdd" (length 4).
Practice Check: Input: s = "abccab", k = 4 → Output: 6
"abccab" has only 3 distinct letters ({'a','b','c'} ≤ 4), so the full string of length 6 qualifies!
Algorithm Summary & HashMap Invariant
O(N) Time • O(k) Space
Tracking Distinct Characters via map.size(): HashMap<Character, Integer> map stores the exact frequency of each character inside s[left .. right]. Whenever map.size() > k, the window holds too many unique characters and shrinks from left++.
Why if (map.get(leftChar) == 0) map.remove(leftChar); Is Essential: Decrementing a character's count to 0 leaves its key inside the HashMap unless explicitly removed. Calling map.remove(leftChar) immediately reduces map.size() by 1 so the shrink loop terminates the exact moment map.size() ≤ k is restored.
Time Complexity
Ο(N)

Each character is inserted into map once by right and removed at most once by left.

Space Complexity
Ο(k)

The HashMap holds at most k + 1 distinct keys at any point during execution.
