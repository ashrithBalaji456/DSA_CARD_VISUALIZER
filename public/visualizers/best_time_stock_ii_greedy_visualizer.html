Problem Statement

Medium / Greedy
LeetCode 122 • Multiple Transactions

Given an array prices where prices[i] is the stock price on day i, you may complete as many buy-and-sell transactions as you like (you must sell the stock before you buy again) to maximize your total profit.

  • • Strategy: Add every positive day-to-day increase (prices[i] > prices[i - 1]).
  • • If prices never rise, totalProfit remains 0.
Ex 1: [7, 1, 5, 3, 6, 4]
→ (5 - 1) + (6 - 3) = Profit 7
Ex 2: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
→ 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = Profit 4
🧠

Algorithm Summary & Complexity Analysis

Greedy O(n) • O(1) Space
✂Why Summing Every Adjacent Increase Is Optimal (Telescoping Sum):

For any rising streak a < b < c, buying at the valley a and selling at the peak c yields (c - a) = (b - a) + (c - b). By greedily adding every positive adjacent difference prices[i] - prices[i - 1] and skipping drops, we capture 100% of every upward slope in a single pass.

TIME COMPLEXITY
O(n)
Single linear scan from i = 1 to n - 1 doing O(1) work per day.
AUXILIARY SPACE
O(1)
Uses only scalar accumulator totalProfit and loop index i.
