Problem Statement Hard
LeetCode 42

Given n non-negative integers representing an elevation map where the width of each bar is 1, compute how much water it can trap after raining.

Example 1:
Input: height = [0,1,0,2,1,0,1,3,2,1,2,1]
Output: 6
The elevation map traps 6 unit blocks of blue rainwater across its valleys.
Example 2:
Input: height = [4,2,0,3,2,5] → Output: 9
Constraints: n == height.length, 1 ≤ n ≤ 2 × 104, 0 ≤ height[i] ≤ 105.
Algorithm Summary & Two-Pointer Invariant
O(1) Aux Space
Per-Column Water Equation: The water held directly above bar i equals the shorter of the tallest walls to its left and right minus its own height:
water[i] = Math.min(maxLeft, maxRight) - height[i]
Why Process the Smaller End First? Whenever height[left] ≤ height[right], there is guaranteed to be a wall on the right at least as tall as leftMax. Thus, the water level at left depends strictly on leftMax alone (and symmetrically on rightMax when height[right] < height[left]), eliminating auxiliary prefix/suffix arrays.
Time Complexity
Ο(N)

Single converging pass; left and right meet in N - 1 steps.

Space Complexity
Ο(1)

Only 5 scalar variables (left, right, leftMax, rightMax, water).
