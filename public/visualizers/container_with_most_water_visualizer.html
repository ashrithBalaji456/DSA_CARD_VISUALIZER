Problem Statement Medium
LeetCode 11

You are given an integer array height of length n. There are n vertical lines drawn such that the two endpoints of the ith line are (i, 0) and (i, height[i]).

Find two lines that together with the x-axis form a container, such that the container contains the most water. Return the maximum amount of water a container can store. Notice that you may not slant the container.

Example 1:
Input: height = [1, 8, 6, 2, 5, 4, 8, 3, 7]
Output: 49
Walls at index 1 (h=8) and index 8 (h=7) yield width 7 × minHeight 7 = 49.
Example 2:
Input: height = [1, 1] → Output: 1
Constraints: 2 ≤ height.length ≤ 105, 0 ≤ height[i] ≤ 104.
Algorithm Summary & Greedy Proof
Two-Pointer Pruning
Water Volume Formula: The water level cannot exceed the shorter boundary wall without spilling over:
area = (right - left) × Math.min(height[left], height[right])
Why Always Move the Shorter Wall? Starting at the widest span (left = 0, right = n - 1), every inward step shrinks width by 1. Keeping the shorter wall and moving the taller wall can never increase minHeight, guaranteeing smaller area. Thus, we safely eliminate the shorter wall (left++ or right--) in search of a taller boundary.
